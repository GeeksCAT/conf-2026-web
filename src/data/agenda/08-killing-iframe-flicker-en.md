Working with iframes is more common and necessary than we think. They are everywhere on the web, and every single one of them shares a painful issue. In this talk, we dive into this limitation with a real-world case study from Framer. In the first half, we explain the problem, why it happens, and how we worked around it creatively.

The second half focuses on the technical side of how browsers render websites and nested iframes, how they communicate, and—most excitingly—we reveal an unreported Chrome bug that even Google's own team didn't know about! A practical, deep-dive session demonstrating that you don't have to be a Google engineer to understand browser internals.
