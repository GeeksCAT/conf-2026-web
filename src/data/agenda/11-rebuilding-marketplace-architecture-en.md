How we rebuilt a 15-year-old marketplace from the architecture up. With dozens of engineers who had left their mark on the codebase, every update made the React application feel more sluggish and harder to maintain.

This talk dives into how we rebuilt the system from the ground up toward an event-driven architecture, exploring patterns, technical decisions, and lessons learned.
