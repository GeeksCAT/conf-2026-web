The current AI ecosystem pushes us into absolute dependency on third parties. When coding, writing, or building applications, we almost always rely on intermediaries we don't control, like Claude or Codex. They are closed cloud black-boxes that can change pricing, update privacy terms, or stop working overnight.

For the geek community, this loss of control is unacceptable. Local AI frees us from these intermediaries and restores digital autonomy: models run on your machine, under your rules. But is it technically viable? Can we get top-tier performance on our own hardware?

In this opening keynote, we combine the philosophy of digital sovereignty with rigorous engineering. Based on Softcatalà's local AI guide and benchmarks, we analyze with real data how technological freedom works in the terminal.

We will explore linguistic accuracy metrics of modern open models, resource consumption, and how optimization techniques (like quantization and Small Language Models / SLMs) enable excellent results efficiently. An essential talk for anyone looking to close the cloud connection and embrace 100% sovereign, free, and transparent AI.
