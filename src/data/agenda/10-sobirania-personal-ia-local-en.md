While Large Language Models have become standard developer tooling, most people consume them via cloud APIs, sending sensitive personal and codebase data to third parties. How can we reclaim digital privacy?

In this lightning talk, we explore running local AI models powerful enough for daily workflows, surveying open models and the optimizations that allow 27B-parameter models to run on consumer hardware and even mobile phones.
