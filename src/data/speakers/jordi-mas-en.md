bio: |
  Jordi Mas is a software engineer and a founding member of Softcatalà. As a developer, he contributes to dozens of free software projects and works on translating software into Catalan in projects such as the GNOME desktop or the GIMP graphic design program. He also builds language resources for translators, like Softcatalà's translation memories. He is the lead developer of Softcatalà's neural translators (18 language pairs, including English to Catalan) and of its audio and video transcription system. Lately he works on evaluating AI in Catalan (LLMs, ASR, embeddings) and on documenting guides for running AI locally in Catalan.
